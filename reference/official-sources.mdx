## Why These Three Sources?

The selection of exactly these three official sources reflects the three knowledge domains that Nexus Ciencia brings together.

### NASA — The Space Domain

NASA publishes mission data and program timelines directly from the agency responsible for Artemis. Using nasa.gov ensures that all information about the Orion capsule and astronaut recovery operations reflects official, government-published releases.

### U.S. Navy — The Military Operations Domain

The Navy's formal role in astronaut recovery operations is operationally critical. navy.mil is the authoritative source for the ships, divers, and MH-60S helicopters that make every Artemis splashdown recovery possible. It also provides the record for Navy programs like Project Vanguard and the NRL's contributions to the founding of NASA Goddard.

### NOAA Ocean Explorer — The Ocean Science Domain

NOAA Ocean Explorer bridges the gap between space exploration and deep-sea science. The same extreme-environment engineering challenges that appear in deep-ocean research reappear in spacecraft design, and the extremophile organisms found near Challenger Deep inform astrobiology models for the subsurface oceans of Europa and Enceladus. NOAA's government-published research makes it the appropriate citation for all ocean and life-science content in the portal.
