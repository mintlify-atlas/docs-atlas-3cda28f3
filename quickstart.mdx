Nexus Ciencia is designed to be immediately usable — no setup, no accounts. This quickstart walks you through the four core actions: opening the portal, sending your first AI message, reading a full knowledge-card analysis, and launching your first arcade game. You'll be up and running in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Portal">
    Open `NEXUS.html` in any modern web browser (locally or from a static host). The page loads instantly with a full-screen hero section.

    At the top of every page you'll find the **fixed navigation bar**, which stays visible as you scroll. It contains five links:

    | Nav Link | Destination |
    |---|---|
    | **Asistente IA** | `#ia-chat` — the AI chat section |
    | **Arcade** | `#juegos` — the arcade game section |
    | **Explicaciones Detalladas** | `#explicaciones` — the knowledge cards |
    | **Hitos 2020-2026** | `#cronologia` — the historical timeline |
    | **Fuentes** | `#fuentes` — official source links |

    A prominent **"Preguntar a la IA"** (Ask the AI) button in the top-right corner of the navbar also scrolls you directly to the chat section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Chat with the AI Assistant">
    Click **Asistente IA** in the navbar or the **Preguntar a la IA** button — both take you to the `#ia-chat` section.

    You'll see a chat window pre-loaded with a greeting from the AI. Type your question into the input field at the bottom and press **Enviar** (Send) or hit **Enter**.

    A good first question to try:

    ```
    ¿Cómo ayuda la Marina a recuperar las naves de la NASA?
    ```

    The assistant replies within milliseconds using its built-in knowledge base, which covers:
    - The NASA–Navy institutional origins
    - Astronaut splashdown and ocean recovery operations
    - The Artemis II lunar flyby mission (2026)
    - The Mariana Trench and extreme-environment technology
    - NEEMO underwater astronaut training

    No internet connection is required for the AI — all responses are generated from the knowledge base embedded directly in the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Expand a Knowledge Card">
    Scroll down to the **Explicaciones Detalladas** section (`#explicaciones`), or click the matching link in the navbar.

    You'll find a 2×2 grid of knowledge cards, each covering one of the four scientific topics:

    1. **¿La Marina creó a la NASA?** — NASA–Navy institutional origins
    2. **Rescatistas del Espacio** — astronaut ocean rescue operations
    3. **El Océano como Análogo Espacial** — NEEMO underwater space training
    4. **Tecnología de la Fosa de las Marianas** — Mariana Trench engineering

    Each card shows a short summary paragraph by default. To read the full analysis, click the **"Leer análisis completo"** button at the bottom of any card. The card expands in place to reveal the complete deep-dive text. Click the same button again (now labelled *"Ocultar análisis"*) to collapse it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Play an Arcade Game">
    Scroll to the **Misiones de Pilotaje** section (`#juegos`), or click **Arcade** in the navbar.

    Select your game mode using the two buttons above the canvas:

    - 🚀 **Misión Espacial** — pilot a rocket through a meteor field in outer space
    - 🌊 **Explorador Abisal** — navigate a deep-sea submersible through underwater obstacles

    Once you've chosen a mode, click the **"¡Comenzar Juego!"** button in the centre of the canvas to start.

    Your goal is to travel as far as possible without hitting an obstacle. Your distance score (in metres) is shown in real time above the canvas. When your craft hits an obstacle, the game ends and displays your final distance — click **"¡Comenzar Juego!"** again to retry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keyboard & Touch Controls

Both arcade games share the same control scheme. Each input applies a single **burst of upward thrust** to your craft — in *Misión Espacial* this lifts the rocket, and in *Explorador Abisal* this causes the submersible to rise toward the surface. Gravity pulls the craft back down continuously between inputs.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **Spacebar** | Apply thrust (desktop) |
| **Mouse click** on the canvas | Apply thrust (desktop / trackpad) |
| **"TOCA AQUÍ PARA IMPULSAR"** button | Apply thrust (mobile tap, shown only on small screens) |

Tap or press repeatedly in short bursts to maintain altitude and thread the gaps between obstacles.

  For the richest AI answers, ask about specific programs by name. Try:

  - **"¿Por qué estudia la NASA la Fosa de las Marianas?"** — to learn how deep-ocean extremophiles inform the search for life on Europa and Enceladus
</Tip>
