Whether you want to understand how the U.S. Navy helped found NASA, how astronauts are rescued from the sea after splashdown, or why the Mariana Trench matters for alien-life research, you'll find detailed explanations, an AI assistant ready to answer your questions, and arcade-style mini-games to make the learning stick.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Portal">
    Visit the Nexus Ciencia web app. The landing page greets you with a hero section and immediate links to all major areas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the AI Assistant">
    Scroll to the **AI Assistant** section (or click *Ask the AI* in the navbar). Type a question about NASA, the Navy, or ocean science and receive an instant answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a Knowledge Card">
    Go to the **Detailed Explanations** section. Click any card — such as *Did the Navy create NASA?* — to expand the full analysis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play a Mission">
    Head to the **Arcade** section. Choose *Space Mission* or *Deep-Sea Explorer*, click **Start Game**, and use the spacebar or screen tap to pilot your craft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Nexus Ciencia runs entirely in the browser — no account, installation, or download is required.
</Note>
